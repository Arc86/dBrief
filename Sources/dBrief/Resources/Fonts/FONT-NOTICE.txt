Default uses the macOS system sans-serif outside Paper modes and the built-in
Georgia font in Paper modes. The explicit San Francisco option always uses
the macOS system sans-serif, including in Paper modes. Inter and OpenDyslexic
fall back to the system sans-serif if font registration or lookup fails.

Lato, Open Sans, Roboto and EB Garamond font files are bundled as additional easy-to-read
reading fonts. They are copied unmodified from the official Google Fonts repository
(https://github.com/google/fonts, branch main, directories ofl/lato, ofl/opensans,
ofl/roboto and ofl/ebgaramond) on 2026-10-11; Open Sans, Roboto and EB Garamond are
the variable fonts, renamed to *-Variable.ttf. All four are licensed under the SIL
Open Font License 1.1; each upstream OFL.txt is included as <Family>-OFL.txt.
