Transcript Viewer · Three design directions

Custom UI inspired by Pen. AI beside the content, with summaries and actions easy to review. Existing app features only.

Existing features and design trade-offs

A · Pen workspace: a pale custom workspace with sidebar smart views, underline document tabs, white summary and action cards, and an AI inspector. Closest to the supplied Pen design.

B · Editorial workspace: a custom shell with warm paper surfaces and editorial typography. More space for reading and reflecting on the meeting.

C · Studio workspace: a custom dark shell with summary and action cards side by side and a separate AI inspector. The two document columns should stack when the window narrows.

Preserved: Summary/Transcript switching, summary copy and editing, spoken summaries, owner-grouped action completion, tags, speaker naming, transcript search, audio playback and seeking, speed, AI prompt templates, chat, copy/read answer, recording search and filters, record, refresh, settings, reprocessing, display options, privacy receipt, deletion, and collapsible/resizable panes.

Scope: Actions stay inside Summary. Reprocessing, display, and privacy commands keep their existing menus and sheets. No sharing, starred recordings, separate Insights tab, speaker percentage analytics, chapter navigation, or folder-management features.

Review status: these are static visual proposals with illustrative meeting content. App behavior has not changed. Choose a direction before implementation. The existing app’s loading, empty, live, and recovery behavior must carry into the selected design.

Mock-up controls select images; controls pictured inside the app are visual only.
