Trade-offs

Option A — Scroll

  • Everything visible at once, just scroll
  • Simpler to implement, no state
  • Familiar — works like any list
  • Popover can feel tall on long content

Option B — Collapsible

  • Compact regardless of content length
  • User controls what they see
  • More interactions to reach content
  • Adds complexity for longer meetings
