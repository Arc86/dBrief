# Appearance & Fonts

Choose how dBrief looks: its theme, accent colour, and the fonts used for menus and for reading your notes.

## Settings → Appearance

### Theme

Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow System**. **Follow System** switches between your light and dark theme with macOS.

Each mode has two themes to pick from:

- **Light mode:** **Light** or **Paper** (a warm, paper-like background)
- **Dark mode:** **Dark** or **Dark Paper**

### Accent

Pick an accent colour (**Blue**, **Violet**, **Green**, **Orange**, **Black**, **White**) or click **Custom…** to choose any colour.

Turn on **Non-neon** to use your accent colour instead of the dBrief brand gradient on outlines and the AI sparkle, for a quieter look.

### Interface text

The **Interface text** card sets the **Font** and **Text size** used throughout dBrief: menus, transcripts and settings. A live preview shows the result.

## Reading fonts

dBrief offers many reading fonts, including ones designed to be easy to read:

- **Easy to read:** Lexend, OpenDyslexic, Verdana, Tahoma, and Dyslexie (when it's installed on your Mac)
- **Sans serif:** San Francisco, Inter, Lato, Open Sans, Roboto, Arial, Helvetica, Calibri, Trebuchet MS
- **Serif:** EB Garamond, Georgia, Times New Roman
- **Monospace**

Choose **Other Font…** to use any font installed on your Mac; a searchable list shows each font in its own style. If you later remove that font, dBrief falls back to the system font and marks it **Not Installed** in the menu.

You'll find the same font menu in three places:

- **Settings → Appearance → Interface text**, for all of dBrief
- The viewer's **Display options** (the text-size icon in the header), under **Reading options**. Here you can also choose **Same as Interface Text**, set the text size and **Transcript density**, and show or hide **Speaker Names**. **Reset reading options** goes back to the defaults.
- The [Focus Reader](../history/focus-reader.md)'s **Text size and font** button, which has its own font, size, line spacing and width

Fonts only change how dBrief shows your notes. Exported Markdown and integrations don't keep the font.
