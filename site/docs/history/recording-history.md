# Recording History

The **Recent recordings** list in the dBrief menu bar window shows your latest recordings.

## Viewing history

Click the dBrief menu bar icon to open the main window. Your recordings appear under **Recent recordings**, sorted by most recent first. The list shows your five latest recordings; when there are more, **Show all in library** opens the rest in the viewer.

The window also has a **Recording library** button (**⌘L**) that opens the full [transcript viewer](transcript-viewer.md) — a two-pane window listing all your recordings — without having to expand a row first.

## Expanding a recording

Each row shows the date, duration, and processing status, with a play button on the left. Click a row to expand it and see its **action chips** — quick actions you can take.

## Action chips

| Chip | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Copy Notes** / **Copy Transcript** | Copy the summary and action items, or the transcript when there's no summary yet |
| **Show in Finder** | Reveal the recording and Markdown files on disk |
| **Reprocess** | Open the reprocessing menu (see below) |
| **Open** | Open the recording in the [transcript viewer](transcript-viewer.md) — summary, transcript, speakers, audio, and chat |
| **Integrations** | Review delivery status and retry integrations |
| **Delete** | Remove the recording and its files, after you confirm |

Right-click a recording for the same actions: **Open**, **Copy Notes**, **Show in Finder**, **Reprocess**, **Integrations…** and **Delete Recording…**. The library sidebar has the same menu, so the actions work the same everywhere.

Each row also shows a status, such as **Not processed**, **Queued**, **Processing**, **Transcribed**, **Analyzed** or **Needs attention**.

Use the play button on the row to play the recording in the inline mini player.

## Re-running transcription or AI analysis

Open **Reprocess** to choose **Retranscribe…**, **Re-run AI Analysis…**, or **Detect Speakers Again…**. See [Reprocessing a Recording](reprocessing.md) for options, restoring previous results, and how exports are handled.

## Older recordings

The menu bar list only shows your most recent recordings. This is a limit on the recent list, not the recording library. Open the viewer to browse and search older recordings. Recording files and Markdown exports remain in your output folder unless you delete them or configured retention removes them.

See [File Locations](../reference/file-locations.md) to find your recordings on disk.

## Search the full library

Open the viewer and use the **Search recordings…** field to find words across transcripts, titles, participants, speakers, tags, action items and their owners, dates, and call apps.

Saved views include **All Recordings**, **Unfinished Actions**, **Failed Jobs**, **Queued / Interrupted**, **Recently Processed**, and **People I Met This Month**. Action-item completion is saved and reflected across views.

Use [Queue & Recovery](queue-recovery.md) to manage pending jobs and failed deliveries, and open a recording's [Privacy receipt](../reference/privacy-receipts.md) to review processing and delivery evidence.
