# Recording History

The history list in the dBrief menu bar window shows your recent recordings.

## Viewing history

Click the dBrief menu bar icon to open the main window. Your recordings appear in the history list, sorted by most recent first. dBrief keeps up to 20 recordings in the list.

The header has an **Open Viewer** button that opens the full [transcript viewer](transcript-viewer.md) — a two-pane window listing all your recordings — without having to expand a row first.

## Expanding a recording

Click a row to expand it. You'll see:

- **Duration** of the recording
- **Action chips** — quick actions you can take

## Action chips

| Chip | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Play** | Play back the recording audio in the inline mini player |
| **Transcript** | Open the [transcript viewer](transcript-viewer.md) — transcript, speakers, audio sync, and chat |
| **Copy** | Copy the summary or transcript to the clipboard |
| **Show in Finder** | Reveal the recording and Markdown files on disk |
| **Re-run AI** | Run AI analysis again |
| **Delete** | Remove the recording and its files |

## Re-running transcription or AI analysis

Open **Reprocess** to choose **Retranscribe…**, **Re-run AI analysis…**, or **Detect speakers again…**. See [Reprocessing a Recording](reprocessing.md) for options, restoring previous results, and how exports are handled.

## The 20-item cap

The history list shows your 20 most recent recordings. This is a limit on the recent list, not the recording library. Open the viewer to browse and search older recordings. Recording files and Markdown exports remain in your output folder unless you delete them or configured retention removes them.

See [File Locations](../reference/file-locations.md) to find your recordings on disk.

## Search the full library

Open the viewer and use the library search to find words across transcripts, titles, participants, speakers, tags, action items and their owners, dates, and call apps.

Saved views include **All Recordings**, **Unfinished Actions**, **Failed Jobs**, **Queued / Interrupted**, **Recently Processed**, and **People I Met This Month**. Action-item completion is saved and reflected across views.

Use [Queue & Recovery](queue-recovery.md) to manage pending jobs and failed deliveries, and open a recording's [Privacy receipt](../reference/privacy-receipts.md) to review processing and delivery evidence.
