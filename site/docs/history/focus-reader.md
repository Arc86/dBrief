# Focus Reader

Read a meeting's notes full screen, in one calm reading column, without the rest of the viewer around them.

## Opening it

Open a recording in the [transcript viewer](transcript-viewer.md), then do one of these:

- Choose **View ▸ Focus Reader**
- Press **⇧⌘F**
- Click the expand icon (two diagonal arrows) in the viewer's header

The Focus Reader opens in full screen with the recording's title, date and duration at the top.

To leave, press **Esc** or **⇧⌘F**, or click the **✕** in the top-right corner. You return to the viewer exactly as you left it.

## Notes and Transcript

The Focus Reader has two pages:

- **Notes** — the summary, action items grouped by owner (done items are marked), and tags
- **Transcript** — the full transcript, one speaker turn after another

Switch with the **Notes / Transcript** control at the top, or press **⌘1** (Notes) and **⌘2** (Transcript). If a recording has only one of the two, the switch is hidden. The Focus Reader is for reading only: edit the summary or speakers in the viewer.

The controls fade after a few seconds so they don't distract. Move the pointer or press **Tab** to bring them back.

## Text size, font and layout

Click the **Aa** button (**Text size and font**) at the top to adjust:

- **Font** — any of dBrief's reading fonts, **Other Font…** for any font installed on your Mac, or **Same as the viewer**
- **Text size** — from 14 to 40 pt
- **Line spacing** — more or less space between lines
- **Width** — **Narrow**, **Medium**, **Wide** or **Full width** column

You can also change the size with **⌘+** and **⌘−**, or pinch on the trackpad.

These choices belong to the Focus Reader only. They don't change the viewer, your exports or your notes, and they're remembered for next time.

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **Space** / **⇧Space** | Scroll down / up a screen |
| **Page Down** / **Page Up** | Scroll a screen |
| **↓** / **↑** | Scroll a little |
| **Home** / **End** | Go to the top / bottom |
| **⌘1** / **⌘2** | Show Notes / Transcript |
| **⌘+** / **⌘−** | Larger / smaller text |
| **Esc** or **⇧⌘F** | Leave the Focus Reader |

See [Keyboard Shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md) for the rest of dBrief.
