How it works

Hit record.
Walk out with the brief.

No setup, no bots in your calls, no "I'll review the recording later." You talk. dBrief handles the rest.

01

Show up. Press one key.

One keystroke starts everything. No dock clutter, no bot joins the call.

  • Ctrl + Option + Cmd + R from anywhere on your Mac
  • Captures mic and system audio — both sides of the call
  • Auto-starts when Zoom, Meet, Teams, Slack or Discord rings in
  • Chosen specifically to avoid Chrome's hard-refresh and Slack's mark-as-read
02

Read every word, instantly.

Minutes, not hours. On your Mac, not in a stranger's data center.

  • Apple Speech, on-device Whisper, or Parakeet TDT
  • Speakers labeled, word-level timestamps, 15+ languages
  • Paste a YouTube link — skip the meeting entirely
03

Get the brief. Keep the brain.

AI writes the summary, your second brain keeps the receipts.

  • Summary, action items, tags, sentiment — generated for you
  • Chat with the transcript — ask "what did I agree to?" and get the answer
  • Title and attendees pulled straight from your calendar
  • Clean Markdown lands in Obsidian, Apple Notes, or wherever
Private by default

Your meetings
never leave your Mac.

No dBrief cloud. No account. No "trust us." The transcript is yours, the audio is yours, and the AI can be too — running on Apple Silicon, or pointed at a model you control.

  • Transcribe with Apple Speech, Whisper or Parakeet — all on-device
  • Summarize with Apple Intelligence or local Gemma 4
  • Want a remote model? Bring your own key — we never see it
  • Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero dark patterns.
