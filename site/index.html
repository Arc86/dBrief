Notes from every meeting, without taking any.

dBrief lives in your menu bar. It records the call (no bot invited), then hands you the brief: what was decided, who's on the hook for what, and a transcript you can simply ask.

Hit record. Nobody gets a bot.

dBrief listens through your own Mac: your microphone and the other side of the call. No assistant joins the meeting, no awkward “who invited the robot?”.

  • Start from the menu bar, or press ⌃⌥⌘R anywhere.
  • Spots Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex and FaceTime, and asks if you'd like to record.
  • Back-to-back? Start the next recording while the last one is still being processed.

Everyone leaves knowing their homework.

dBrief sorts the action items by person, quietly drops the “no action items identified” filler, and sends the real ones to wherever your to-do list already lives.

  • Todoist
  • TickTick
  • Notion
  • Apple Reminders
  • Apple Notes
  • Obsidian
  • Webhook

Retries won't create duplicate tasks in Todoist, TickTick or Notion, so a flaky connection won't leave you with the same task three times.

Missed something? Ask the meeting.
