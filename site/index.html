How it works

From recording to notes.

Record from the menu bar, then choose what dBrief should do with the audio. A transcript is enough for some meetings; others need a summary and action items.

01

Start recording

Use the menu bar or a keyboard shortcut. With permission, dBrief records both your mic and the call audio.

  • Ctrl + Option + Cmd + R from anywhere on your Mac
  • Captures mic and system audio for both sides of a call
  • Optional call detection for Zoom, Teams, Meet, Slack, Webex, and FaceTime
  • Start another recording while the last one is processing
02

Read the transcript

Pick an on-device transcription engine if you want the audio processed on your Mac.

  • Apple Speech, on-device Whisper, or Parakeet TDT
  • Speaker labels and timestamps, with language support depending on your engine
  • Transcribe a YouTube or other supported video link without recording it yourself
03

Review and send the notes

Turn on AI analysis when you want a summary, action items, tags, or sentiment.

  • Ask questions about a transcript, such as "What did I agree to?"
  • Use calendar details to help name the meeting and its participants
  • Send notes to Obsidian or Apple Notes, and action items to Apple Reminders
  • Search past recordings, resume interrupted work, or reprocess a recording
Privacy

Choose where your meeting is processed.

dBrief stores recordings on your Mac and has no account or cloud service of its own. Use local engines to keep transcription and analysis on your Mac. If you choose a remote endpoint or a cloud-connected CLI, that service receives the content it needs.

  • Transcribe with Apple Speech, Whisper, or Parakeet on your Mac
  • Summarize with Apple Intelligence or local Gemma 4
  • Choose a remote model, CLI tool, or export when you want to send content elsewhere
  • Check each recording's privacy receipt to see which processing and delivery steps ran

Calendar matching

Use your calendar details

With Calendar access, dBrief can match a recording to a meeting in your selected calendars. You can use its title and participant names, or change the match later.
